Original adapted one-shot for four level-8 PCs, approximately 3-4 hours. No Mimics.

## Current canon: v1.2 jewelry-store finale and cinema projector

- The projector is physically in the CINEMA PROJECTION BOOTH, in the booth at B2-D3. Bombeldo hosted a movie screening and fled to The Dungeoneer after the projector overloaded.
- The cinema screen is the Thornwatch return route. STOP opens it for one minute; no escaped monster vanishes. An intact projector can be restarted and stopped to reopen a return window.
- Young Red Dragon begins inside **Coronet Jewelers** at **AB11-AC12** and hoards gold, stones and watches. The jeweler occupies **Y10-AG14** beside the food court.
- Two independent Owlbears scavenge food-service stockrooms at **L19-M20** and **Y19-Z20** and enter the food court before the dragon emerges to protect its treasure.
- Both player and DM PNGs were revised to match this layout, with exact footprints and shop positioning.
- The old v7 PDF is superseded by v8. The live adventure JSON and hosted maps are the canonical versions.
- The adventure remains free-roaming: characters can find the dragon or stop the projector early. Do not introduce new monsters or force them through fixed scenes.
