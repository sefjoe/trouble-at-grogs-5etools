# Chaos at the Food Court - Modern Earth

Original adapted one-shot for four level-8 PCs, approximately 3-4 hours. No Mimics.

5etools raw import URL:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sefjoe/trouble-at-grogs-5etools/main/one-shots/chaos-at-the-food-court/Chaos_at_the_Food_Court_5etools.json

Maps: mall_player.png and mall_dm.png. Both are actual PNG files, 816 x 528 pixels, 34 x 22 squares at 24 pixels per square. DM map uses H hydra, S spectator, D1/D2 displacers, C cube, B bulette, O1/O2 owlbears, R red dragon. Keep DM overlay hidden from players. Four PCs, nine enemies.

All stat links resolve to built-in Monster Manual 2024 source XMM when it is available in the current 5etools installation. The JSON is a new adventure; existing repo files are unchanged.

These files were structurally created and verified as GitHub assets; live browser import into the user's particular 5etools install is still a separate check.

## Current canon: v1.2 jewelry-store finale and cinema projector

- The projector is physically in the CINEMA PROJECTION BOOTH, in the booth at B2-D3. Bombeldo hosted a movie screening and fled to The Dungeoneer after the projector overloaded.
- The cinema screen is the Thornwatch return route. STOP opens it for one minute; no escaped monster vanishes. An intact projector can be restarted and stopped to reopen a return window.
- Young Red Dragon begins inside **Coronet Jewelers** at **AB11-AC12** and hoards gold, stones and watches. The jeweler occupies **Y10-AG14** beside the food court.
- Two independent Owlbears scavenge food-service stockrooms at **L19-M20** and **Y19-Z20** and enter the food court before the dragon emerges to protect its treasure.
- Both player and DM PNGs were revised to match this layout, with exact footprints and shop positioning.
- The old v7 PDF is superseded by v8. The live adventure JSON and hosted maps are the canonical versions.
- The adventure remains free-roaming: characters can find the dragon or stop the projector early. Do not introduce new monsters or force them through fixed scenes.

## Current canon: v1.3.1 resonance and jewelry-shop theft

- No missing Thornwatch travelers or wagons. The reeve hired adventurers to investigate odd lights, humming, and reflections around the arch.
- Nine creatures arrived through secondary planar tears at once. Living creatures that remain within mall grounds produce resonant feedback that keeps the PROJECTOR CRYSTAL shining, even when its motor is unplugged or STOP is pulled. The characters entered by the primary cinema screen and are not resonant anchors.
- Disconnect a living monster anchor by death, removal to another plane, or leaving mall property. The screen cannot become a stable passage home until every link disconnects. Then STOP opens one 60-second window (or automatically if it was already set to STOP). The machine can be restarted and stopped when no anchors remain.
- Destroying the crystal destroys the controllable exit and does not banish any monster. Conventional electrical shutdown never banishes a monster.
- Eddie Vale is an ordinary greedy shopper. While Owlbears cause chaos, he notices broken glass at Coronet Jewelers, steals jewelry, and runs into the food court. The Young Red Dragon chases the thief to reclaim its hoard, unless the party prevents or changes those events.
- The dragon is always inside Coronet Jewelers before it reacts. The movie projector remains inside the CINEMA PROJECTION BOOTH.
- Revised adventure text is v1.3.1. The v8 printable DM packet is superseded by the current JSON; use the latest v9 book when available.
