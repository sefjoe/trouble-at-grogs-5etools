# The Hag's Dinner Party
**Current adventure:** V13.4 · **System:** D&D 2024 (levels 2–3) · **Format:** Hall of Heroes three-hour one-shot

Rescue nine-year-old Mira from a haunted house where a dead hag's enchanted dinner ritual continues to imprison guests. The upstairs demiplane presents four discovery-assigned rooms—Guest Bedroom, Dressing Room, Music Room, and Playroom—before the Flesh Golem, two Skeleton servants, and optionally two reduced-strength Dinner Specters confront the party at dinner.

## Running
1. Import the V13.4 JSON in 5etools using its homebrew import workflow; replace earlier HDP versions to avoid duplicates.
2. Use the DM map and terrain overlay to build the single stationary 36×24 mat. The ground floor is narrated; upstairs and final dining room share the mat.
3. Read the adventure's *DM front card* and encounter clock before play.
4. If an adaptive pacing trigger reveals Mira early, every **still-unrevealed** room is empty. Rooms already revealed remain furnished.
5. Optional combat escalation: two Dinner Specters, 11 HP each; Spectral Touch +4, 1d6 Necrotic and **no maximum-HP reduction**. They vanish when the Flesh Golem and two Skeleton guardians are defeated/neutralized.
